Support

Importing Media

Tap the import button in PianoKeys to choose an audio or video file from Files. You can also share supported audio or video files to PianoKeys from other apps.

TikTok Links

PianoKeys can save a TikTok link as a local reference. It does not automatically download third-party media. To generate notes, import or share an audio or video file you own or have permission to use.

Playback and Practice

Open a ready project to use falling notes, note labels, left/right-hand colors, sheet preview, audio mode controls, and practice progress tracking.

Deleting Data

Delete individual projects from the library context menu. Delete the app from your device to remove all local PianoKeys data.
