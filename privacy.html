PianoKeys

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Data Collection

PianoKeys does not collect personal data, does not sell personal data, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Imported Media

Audio and video files imported into PianoKeys are processed on device. Generated notes, practice progress, exported MIDI/MusicXML files, and synthesized piano audio are stored locally on the device.

PianoKeys can save a TikTok link as a local reference, but it does not download third-party media automatically. Users are responsible for importing or sharing only media they own or have permission to use.

Sharing and Export

If you choose to export MIDI or MusicXML, iOS presents the system share sheet. The destination you choose controls how that exported file is handled.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual projects in PianoKeys. You can also remove all local PianoKeys data by deleting the app from your device.

Third-Party Technology

PianoKeys includes Spotify Basic Pitch, an on-device music transcription model, under the Apache License 2.0. The model runs locally in the app.

Contact

Use the App Store support link for privacy or support questions.
